Changelog · 2026

Every release, and what you can download.

The app is at 1.4.0, which is the store-ready build. The Play listing is not live yet, so the newest signed APK you can install today is 1.3.0.

1.4.0

22 November 2026 store-ready · not downloadable
  • Android 16 (API 36) target, signed AAB path, 16 KB page-size alignment, adaptive launcher icon.
  • WebView hardening: no reflected JavaScript bridge, HTTPS-only intents, file and content access off, mixed content never, third-party cookies off, Safe Browsing on.
  • Download-size confirmation for neural voices and filter lists.
  • Diagnostics and crash logs redacted before they are written or shared.
  • Reader: 56 dp mini player, two-column library at tablet widths, reduced-motion support, 1.3×/2× text-scale layouts.

1.3.0

4 November 2026 latest APK
  • Supertonic 3 and ZipVoice join Kokoro and Pocket TTS.
  • Faster first audio: the engine is prewarmed when content is queued, and up to 8 s / 4 sentences are prerendered while playback is held.
  • Playback survives pause, calls and screen changes; a blocking write no longer kills the PCM writer.
  • Ad text stays out of the reader: blocks whose only live match is hidden are dropped.
  • An on-device TTS benchmark activity, with committed emulator and phone results.
Download the APK (15.5 MB) SHA-256

1.2.1

27 October 2026 superseded
  • Fixes for extraction and playback regressions reported after 1.2.0.

1.2.0

27 October 2026 superseded
  • Home tab, share-sheet intake, library with continue reading, bookmarks and reading statistics.
  • Sleep timer, reader typography, offline WAV capture and a diagnostics bundle.
  • EPUB, PDF (text layer) and plain text import.

1.1.0

24 October 2026 superseded
  • Reader mode per site, ad blocking, and voice previews.

1.0.0

22 October 2026 superseded
  • First release: browser, extraction, system TTS and Kokoro, screen-off playback.

Older APKs are not re-signed. Install only from this repository’s releases, and check the SHA-256 beside each file.
