Effective 7 November 2026 NovelSpeak 1.4.0+

Privacy Policy

NovelSpeak is a local-first web novel reader for Android. This policy explains exactly what the app does with data — and what it deliberately does not do.

Short version: NovelSpeak has no account, no ads, no analytics and no telemetry. Your reading position, library, imported books, bookmarks and settings stay in the app's private storage on your device. The app only talks to the network to load pages you ask it to open, download a voice model or filter list when you explicitly confirm it, and open Google Search or Google Translate when you choose those actions. Nothing is uploaded to a NovelSpeak server because there is no NovelSpeak server.

1. Who we are

NovelSpeak is developed by Vipul Gote, an individual Android developer (India). In this policy, “NovelSpeak”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to that developer, and “the app” refers to the NovelSpeak Android application distributed on Google Play.

Privacy and data inquiries can be submitted through the public issue tracker: github.com/vipulgote1999/NovelSpeak/issues. Please use the title “Privacy request” so it is routed correctly. You can also use the developer contact email shown on the Google Play listing.

2. What the app stores on your device

All of the following is stored locally in the app's private sandbox (or in Android's Downloads collection when you explicitly export a file):

  • Reading state: current URL, reading position, sentence index, playback speed, sleep-timer state and reader preferences.
  • Library: chapter history, continue-reading entries, bookmarks and reading statistics. These are local records only.
  • Imported books: text extracted from EPUB/PDF/text files you choose through Android's document picker. The original file is not copied; the extracted text is stored in app-private storage.
  • Voice and display settings: selected engine/voice, pronunciation rules, theme, font size, ad-block preferences and per-site settings.
  • Downloaded resources: optional neural voice model files and bundled / updated ad-block filter lists.
  • WebView data: cookies, local storage and cache created by websites you open. These belong to those websites and are governed by their policies. NovelSpeak disables third-party cookies and does not read page form fields or credentials.
  • Diagnostics: a capped local event log and last-crash file used only when you tap “Share diagnostics”. The bundle is created on-device and handed to the app you pick through Android's share sheet; NovelSpeak never uploads it.

3. Network destinations

NovelSpeak makes network requests only for these purposes:

  • Websites you open. Pages, images and other resources are fetched directly from the site you navigate to, exactly as a browser would. Those sites receive your IP address, user agent and any cookies they set, under their own privacy policies. NovelSpeak does not proxy, log or upload your browsing history to the developer.
  • Google Search. If you type a search query instead of a URL, the app opens a Google Search results page with your query. This is a normal navigation to Google, not an API call from the app.
  • Google Translate. If you tap “Translate page”, the app opens Google Translate with the current page address so the page can be translated. This is a user-initiated navigation to Google.
  • Neural voice models. When you tap Install and confirm the displayed download size, the app downloads the official model archive from the k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx GitHub releases. The archive is verified with SHA-256 before extraction. The app does not download executable code; the archives contain model data, tokens and voice files.
  • Ad-block filter lists. When you confirm “Update lists”, the app downloads filter lists from EasyList and uBlock Origin sources. These are text data, not executable code.

NovelSpeak does not contact an update server, analytics endpoint, advertising network, crash-reporting service or push-notification service. There is no advertising ID, device fingerprinting or cross-app tracking.

4. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent or share personal and sensitive user data. The only transfers off the device are:

  • the normal web requests described in section 3, made to sites and services you choose;
  • a diagnostics zip that you explicitly hand to another app through Android's share sheet; you choose the recipient; and
  • a file you explicitly export (for example a WAV reading) to Android's Downloads collection, where you control it.

5. Permissions

  • INTERNET — load pages, download an optional voice model and update filter lists.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — check connectivity before a model or filter-list download.
  • WAKE_LOCK — keep audio playback alive while the screen is off.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK — run read-aloud playback as a media foreground service with lock-screen controls.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — show the playback notification on Android 13+. It is requested at first playback and is optional; denying it does not block playback.

NovelSpeak does not request the microphone, camera, location, contacts, calendar, SMS, call log, body sensors, Bluetooth, storage or “all files access” permissions. Imported documents are opened only through Android's document picker or a share/open intent that you initiate.

6. Security

  • All app-initiated network traffic uses HTTPS; Android's network security configuration blocks cleartext traffic in the Play release.
  • Downloaded voice models are verified with SHA-256 before use.
  • The app does not download or execute dex, JAR or native code at runtime.
  • Exported diagnostics are created in app-private cache storage and shared only through a read-only content provider with a path-traversal guard.
  • No account credentials are stored by NovelSpeak because the app has no account system.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • clear the app's storage in Android Settings → Apps → NovelSpeak → Storage → Clear data;
  • uninstall the app, which removes its private storage;
  • delete exported files from Downloads using your file manager; or
  • delete individual imported books, bookmarks or library entries inside the app.

Because NovelSpeak has no account and no server, there is no account deletion request to submit and no server-side copy to erase. If you believe the developer holds data about you, contact us through the issue tracker and we will investigate.

8. Children and unrestricted web access

NovelSpeak is a general-purpose browser/reader with an address bar and can open arbitrary websites. It is not directed to children. The app is intended for users aged 18 and over, and Google Play's target-audience declaration selects 18+ with minor access restricted. Parents and guardians should use Android's parental controls and a filtered browser if a child needs web access. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Copyright and third-party content

NovelSpeak does not host, publish or distribute novels, books or other copyrighted works. It displays pages from websites that you choose to visit, like a browser. The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any website, publisher or author. You are responsible for using the app only with content you are entitled to access. Rights holders can contact us through the issue tracker.

10. Ad blocking

Ad blocking is applied only inside NovelSpeak's own WebView. The app does not modify other apps, system settings, device-wide DNS or network traffic, and it does not interfere with ads shown by other applications. You can turn ad blocking off per site.
