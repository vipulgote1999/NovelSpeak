The Lantern

Marshal Rennick set the lantern on the table. "You came alone."

The woman in the grey coat didn't sit. "You said to."

"I said not to bring the boy."

"He isn't a boy anymore."

From the doorway, a third voice: "He's right here."

The marshal's hand moved to his holster. "Tomas. I told you to wait outside."

The young man stepped in, rain dripping from his sleeves. "And I told you I wouldn't."

Mara looked from one man to the other. "If you two are finished, there's a body in the stable."

Rennick turned to her. "Whose?"

"Mine," said a voice from the yard.
